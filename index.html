How It Works

The VillagKart Journey

From order to delivery — a seamless rural commerce experience powered by technology and local networks.

01

Customer Orders

Rural customers browse and order products through the VillagKart mobile app or ONDC-enabled buyer platform.

02

Mandal Hub Processes

Orders are received at the nearest Mandal-level business hub for processing, inventory allocation, and dispatch.

03

Village Delivery

Tata Ace delivery units carry out last-mile delivery directly to villages, ensuring efficient and timely fulfillment.

04

Customer Delight

Customers receive quality products at competitive prices, with digital receipts and order tracking via the app.
