VillagKart Launches Hub & Spoke Model Across Mandals

VillagKart Team 1 March 2024 247 views

VillagKart has officially launched its revolutionary Hub & Spoke distribution model across multiple Mandals in India, creating over 100 direct and indirect jobs within the first six months of operations.

The Hub & Spoke model places a central Mandal-level Business Hub at the core, connected to surrounding villages through efficient last-mile delivery routes operated by Tata Ace mobile units and local retail partners.

Key Highlights

  • 100+ direct and indirect jobs per Mandal hub
  • 12 product categories including groceries, FMCG, electronics, and agricultural inputs
  • Tata Ace fleet for reliable last-mile village delivery
  • Digital ordering via VillagKart mobile app and ONDC platform

The initiative marks a significant step toward VillagKart's vision of becoming India's most powerful rural commerce and distribution ecosystem.
