Empowering Rural Women Entrepreneurs Through VillagKart

VillagKart Team 15 March 2024 135 views

VillagKart's women entrepreneurship program is helping hundreds of rural women build sustainable businesses as retailers, distributors, and local commerce agents — transforming livelihoods across villages in India.

The program provides training, mentoring, business setup support, and access to VillagKart's digital platform, enabling women to manage their own micro-retail operations or serve as VillagKart agents in their communities.

Program Highlights

  • 500+ women entrepreneurs enrolled and active
  • Training in digital ordering, inventory, and customer service
  • Access to VillagKart's supply chain and pricing
  • Community network for shared learning and support
  • Financial inclusion through digital payment tools

The initiative aligns with VillagKart's broader mission of creating sustainable economic opportunities in rural India while supporting gender equity and inclusion.
