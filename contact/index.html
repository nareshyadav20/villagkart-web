FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is VillagKart?
VillagKart is a technology-driven rural commerce platform connecting villages with manufacturers, suppliers, retailers, and digital marketplaces through a Hub & Spoke model.
How can I become a retail partner?
You can apply through the Partners page or contact us at support@villagkart.com. We'll guide you through the onboarding process.
What is the Hub & Spoke model?
Each Mandal acts as a central hub with surrounding villages as spokes. Tata Ace units deliver products from hubs to villages, ensuring efficient last-mile logistics.
What is ONDC integration?
VillagKart integrates with the Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC), allowing rural customers to access products from multiple national sellers through one app.
How many jobs does VillagKart create?
Within six months of operations at each Mandal hub, VillagKart aims to create approximately 100 direct and indirect jobs in the rural area.
How can farmers partner with VillagKart?
Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and Farmer Producer Companies (FPCs) can partner with VillagKart to market agricultural products through digital channels and our distribution network.
